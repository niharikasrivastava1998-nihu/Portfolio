01 — About

The Soul Behind
the Stories

I'm Niharika Srivastava — a communications professional with the instincts of a writer and the discipline of a strategist. Based in Pune, Maharashtra, I work at the intersection of language, brand building, community, and meaning.

With a Bachelor's in English Literature and a PG Diploma in German from Delhi University, I've built a career where editorial sensitivity meets measurable business outcomes. As a Communications Manager at Bajaj Finance Limited, I work on communities, campaigns, and narratives that scale without losing clarity or warmth.

Beyond work, I'm a published author and a reflective observer of everyday life. My book "To be Continued..." comes from the same place my best work does: attention, emotional honesty, and the belief that words can leave people clearer, calmer, and more connected than before.

I don't believe in content for content's sake.

I believe in language that carries intention, strategy that respects people, and storytelling that leaves a mark.

How I Work

Editorial instinct

I look for the sentence, tone, and structure that make a message feel true before it tries to sell.

Strategic clarity

I enjoy creative work most when it is anchored in audience insight, business goals, and measurable outcomes.

Human communication

Whether I am writing for a brand, a reader, or a community, I care about resonance, trust, and emotional intelligence.
